# Critical factors in the reconstruction of 3D models using the Roofer algorithm: an analysis of geometric and topological consistency in mountainous cities

Based on the experimental process carried out, characterized by multiple iterations and adjustments to both algorithm parameters and input data, a methodological workflow was established that coherently organizes the stages of the reconstruction process, as well as the decision points and adjustments required for its improvement.

This workflow represents an operational synthesis of the main factors that influence the performance of the process, enabling an understanding of how different variables affect the geometric consistency and topological validity of the generated models. In this context, aspects such as the quality and coherence of input building footprints, the accuracy of LiDAR point cloud classification, the influence of vegetation on data availability, the estimation of base height in areas with topographic variability, and the presence of specific construction characteristics are considered.

Within the methodological workflow, tasks are not executed in a linear manner, but rather in a cyclical way. Each iteration allows:

* Evaluating the obtained results
* Identifying geometric and topological inconsistencies
* Adjusting parameters or refining input data
*Re-running the process under updated conditions

This approach makes it possible to isolate the effect of each variable and progressively improve the quality of the final model, ensuring traceability of the applied configurations.
